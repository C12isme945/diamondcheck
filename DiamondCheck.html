How the audit works

Fit a StandardScaler and Ridge model (alpha 10) on 1980–2011 target seasons. Calibrate an absolute-error interval on 2012–2015. Evaluate once on eligible 2016–2025 seasons. Scaling, fitting and the interval never see test outcomes. Features and settings were fixed before the first holdout evaluation.

The six inputs are prior runs/game, on-base percentage, slugging percentage, walks/at-bats, strikeouts/at-bats and the prior league runs/game. Match franchises across immediately adjacent years. Both years need at least 100 games: 2020 and 2021 are excluded, as are expansion transitions without a preceding season.

Intervals use the finite-sample 90% absolute-error quantile from calibration. Era changes and correlated teams violate simple exchangeability assumptions, so the report shows empirical test coverage. These are retrospective forecasts from revised historical data, not a live prospective trial or a 2026 forecast.
